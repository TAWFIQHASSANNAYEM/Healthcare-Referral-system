About the System

Our AI-powered healthcare referral system helps General Practitioners find the most suitable hospitals for their patients based on:

  • Medical department requirements
  • Patient location and distance
  • Hospital capacity and emergency services
  • Real-time availability

The system ensures patients receive timely care at appropriate facilities across Bangladesh.
